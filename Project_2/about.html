Meet Our Team

The team behind ABC'S Digital Payment Platform, inspired by PayPal and designed to provide secure, fast and reliable online financial services.

About The Project

ABC'S is a PayPal-inspired digital payment platform developed as an academic project. The system allows users to register, manage wallets, send funds, receive payments, complete KYC verification, and manage transactions through a secure interface.
